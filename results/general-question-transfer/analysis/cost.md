## Judge throughput (MEASURED, 2026-10-23)

Measured on PimientoRojo by `provenance/judge_measurement_2026-10-23.md` (llama.cpp b11836, Vulkan, `--parallel 1`, `cache_prompt=false`, 24 distinct prompts of ~600 tokens, one request at a time; 12 bench items, no study data). Seconds per call are means; calls/hour = 3600 / mean.

| judge / mode | mean s/call | median s | calls/hour | usable? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| llama-3.3-70b-instruct-q8_0 `logprob_0_100` | 3.82 | 3.71 | 942 | yes: digit mass >= 0.999 on 48/48, 20 top_logprobs returned |
| llama-3.3-70b-instruct-q8_0 `text_0_100` | 6.09 | 5.97 | 591 | yes (not the plan; 1.6x slower, coarse 4-value scores) |
| gpt-oss-20b-mxfp4 `text_0_100 (reasoning low)` | 1.00 | 0.94 | 3600 | yes (its logprob mode is NOT usable: first token `<|channel|>`, digit mass 0 on 48/48) |

Parallelism gives at most 1.3x (gpt-oss, `--parallel 4`; the 70B logprob workload does not scale at all), so the figures are `--parallel 1`, serial. The two judges share one GPU: treat their hours as additive.

**Default design, measured speeds**: primary 278460 calls x 3.82 s = **295.5 h**; secondary 189756 calls x 1.00 s = **52.7 h**; together **348.2 h (14.5 days)**. It fits no schedule short of two weeks, which is why the /8 design below exists.

## The /8 design (PREREG sections 4 and 10)

Target: total judge work about 1/8 of the default's 348.2 h = 43.5 h. Every row below is computed by `div8_plan`; counts include the 5% determinism re-send.

| design | generations | GPU h (1 A40, tokens / s-per-gen) | primary calls | primary h | secondary calls | secondary h | total judge h | vs default |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| default | 80400 | 49.2 / 41.0 | 278460 | 295.5 | 189756 | 52.7 | 348.2 | 1 |
| div8_main | 13200 | 8.1 / 6.7 | 33264 | 35.3 | 7182 | 2.0 | 37.3 | 1/9.3 |
| div8_alt | 17200 | 10.5 / 8.8 | 30408 | 32.3 | 6468 | 1.8 | 34.1 | 1/10.2 |
| div8_main_secondary_100pct_heldout | 13200 | 8.1 / 6.7 | 33264 | 35.3 | 16443 | 4.6 | 39.9 | 1/8.7 |

What each design is:

* `div8_main` - main: {-3,0,+3} for every arm, 3 samples, pairwise on held-out at +-3.
* `div8_alt` - alternative: as main but no pairwise anywhere and 5 samples on the held-out set.
* `div8_main_secondary_100pct_heldout` - main: {-3,0,+3} for every arm, 3 samples, pairwise on held-out at +-3 (secondary judge on ALL held-out rows).

Per question set (`div8_main`; points = steered arm x k points + the shared k=0 baseline; the unhooked byte-identity check is one extra generation-only point on the held-out set):
